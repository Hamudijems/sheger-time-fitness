Membership Questions

What membership plans do you offer?

We offer several membership options to suit different needs:

  • Standard Membership: Full access to gym facilities during regular hours
  • Premium Membership: Full access to gym facilities, group classes, and sauna/steam room
  • Ultimate Membership: All Premium benefits plus 2 personal training sessions monthly
  • Student/Senior: Discounted rates with valid ID
  • Day Pass: Single-day access for visitors

Visit our pricing page for current rates and special offers.

Is there a joining fee?

Yes, there is a one-time joining fee of $49 for new members. However, we frequently run promotions that waive this fee. Check our current offers or speak with a membership consultant to learn about any active promotions.

Do you offer family memberships?

Yes, we offer family membership packages for households with 2 or more members. Each additional family member receives a 15% discount off their membership rate. All family members must reside at the same address, and proof may be required.

Can I freeze my membership temporarily?

Yes, members can freeze their membership for 1-3 months per calendar year for reasons such as medical issues, extended travel, or temporary relocation. A small monthly maintenance fee of $10 applies during the freeze period. Membership freezes must be requested at least 5 days before your billing date and require completion of a membership freeze form.

How do I cancel my membership?

To cancel your membership, we require a 30-day written notice. You can submit your cancellation request in person at the front desk or via email to memberships@powerfit.com. Please note that we do not process cancellations over the phone. Any outstanding balance must be paid before cancellation can be finalized.

Facilities & Hours

What are your hours of operation?

Our gym hours are:

  • Monday - Thursday: 5:00 AM - 11:00 PM
  • Friday: 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Saturday: 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM
  • Sunday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM

Please note that hours may vary during holidays, and we recommend checking our website or social media for any updates to our schedule.

Do you have parking available?

Yes, we offer free parking for members in our dedicated lot. During peak hours (4-7 PM weekdays), the lot can fill up quickly, so we also have an arrangement with the adjacent office building for overflow parking after 6 PM on weekdays and all day on weekends.

Do you have showers and locker rooms?

Yes, we provide fully-equipped locker rooms with showers, toilets, sinks, and changing areas. Amenities include complimentary shampoo, body wash, blow dryers, and towel service (Premium and Ultimate memberships). Day lockers are free to use during your workout, and rental lockers are available for monthly fees.

What equipment do you have?

Our gym features state-of-the-art equipment including:

  • Comprehensive free weight area with multiple squat racks, bench press stations, and dumbbells up to 150lbs
  • Full range of pin-loaded and plate-loaded strength machines
  • Cardio section with treadmills, ellipticals, stair climbers, rowing machines, and stationary bikes
  • Dedicated functional training area with TRX, kettlebells, medicine balls, and more
  • Specialized zones for stretching and mobility work

Our equipment is regularly maintained and updated to ensure the best workout experience.

Is your facility accessible to people with disabilities?

Yes, our facility is ADA compliant with accessible entrances, elevators, locker rooms, and equipment. We have specialized equipment designed for wheelchair users and staff trained to assist members with various disabilities. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us, and we'll be happy to provide more detailed information.

Classes & Services

Are group fitness classes included with membership?

Group fitness classes are included with Premium and Ultimate memberships. Standard members can purchase class packs or pay a drop-in fee per class. We offer over 50 classes weekly, including yoga, HIIT, cycling, strength training, and more. Check our class schedule on our app or website for the most current offerings.

How do I sign up for classes?

You can reserve your spot in classes through our PowerFit mobile app, website portal, or at the front desk. Classes can be booked up to 7 days in advance, and we recommend early registration as popular classes fill up quickly. If you can't attend a class you've signed up for, please cancel at least 2 hours in advance to avoid a late cancellation fee.

How much does personal training cost?

Personal training rates vary based on the trainer's experience and the package you select:

  • Single sessions range from $60-90
  • 5-session packages range from $275-400
  • 10-session packages range from $500-750

New members receive a complimentary fitness consultation and workout plan with a trainer. Visit our personal training page for more details.

Do you offer nutrition counseling?

Yes, we offer nutrition counseling services with our certified nutritionists and dietitians. Services include personalized nutrition plans, meal planning assistance, body composition analysis, and ongoing support. These services are available as standalone offerings or can be combined with personal training for a comprehensive fitness approach. Learn more on our nutrition counseling page.

Do you have childcare services?

Yes, we offer supervised childcare services for children ages 6 months to 12 years. The Kids Club is available during select hours:

  • Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM
  • Saturday: 8:00 AM - 1:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed

Childcare services can be added to any membership for an additional monthly fee or used on a drop-in basis. Reservations are recommended during peak hours.

Getting Started

I'm new to fitness. How do I get started?

Welcome to your fitness journey! Here's how we recommend getting started:

  1. Schedule a complimentary fitness consultation with one of our trainers
  2. Attend our New Member Orientation to learn about the facilities and equipment
  3. Try our "Fitness Foundations" beginner-friendly classes
  4. Consider starting with a few personal training sessions to build confidence
  5. Download our app to track workouts and access beginner workout plans

Our staff is always available to answer questions and provide guidance as you begin your fitness journey.

Do you offer a gym orientation?

Yes, we provide complimentary gym orientations for all new members. During this 30-minute session, a staff member will give you a tour of the facility, explain how to use key equipment, review gym etiquette, and answer any questions you may have. Orientations can be scheduled at the front desk or through our app.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Absolutely! We offer several ways to experience PowerFit before committing to a membership:

  • One-day free guest pass (available through our website)
  • Three-day trial pass for $25 (applicable to membership if you join)
  • Attend an open house event (held monthly)

To arrange a visit, simply fill out the form on our website or stop by the front desk with a valid ID.

What should I bring for my first workout?

For your first visit, we recommend bringing:

  • Comfortable workout clothes
  • Athletic shoes (closed-toe)
  • Water bottle (we have filling stations throughout the facility)
  • Lock for the day-use lockers (or you can purchase one at our pro shop)
  • Towel (provided for Premium and Ultimate members, available for rent for Standard members)
  • Valid ID for check-in

If you're attending a specific class, check if any special equipment is needed, though most classes provide all necessary equipment.

I have a medical condition. Can I still work out?

Many people with various medical conditions can safely exercise with proper guidance. We recommend:

  • Consulting your healthcare provider before starting any exercise program
  • Informing our fitness staff about your condition during your initial consultation
  • Considering our Medical Exercise program, designed specifically for individuals with health concerns
  • Working with one of our certified Special Populations Trainers who have additional training in working with clients with medical conditions

We're committed to helping you find safe and effective ways to improve your health through appropriate exercise.

Gym Policies

What is your guest policy?

Members may bring guests to the gym under these guidelines:

  • Premium and Ultimate members receive 3 free guest passes per month
  • Standard members can bring guests for a fee of $15 per visit
  • The same guest can visit up to 3 times per month
  • Guests must be accompanied by the member and sign a waiver
  • Guests must provide valid ID

Guest privileges may be limited during peak hours at management's discretion.

What is the age requirement for gym use?

Our age policies are as follows:

  • Members must be at least 16 years old to use the gym facilities without supervision
  • Youth ages 13-15 may use the gym when directly supervised by a parent/guardian or during designated teen hours
  • Children 12 and under can participate in Kids Club activities and youth-specific programs only
  • Some specialized equipment and areas may have additional age restrictions for safety

We also offer specific youth fitness programs and family workout times.

What is your cell phone policy?

We ask members to follow these guidelines regarding cell phone use:

  • Phone calls should be taken in the lobby or designated areas, not on the fitness floor
  • Photography or video recording is prohibited in locker rooms and restricted in workout areas to respect members' privacy
  • Music must be listened to with headphones
  • Limited phone use is permitted for fitness tracking apps or viewing workout instructions
  • Please be considerate and limit screen time while using equipment during busy periods
What happens if I forget my key card/fob?

If you forget your membership key card/fob, you can still access the gym by:

  • Checking in at the front desk with a valid photo ID
  • Using our mobile app's digital check-in feature
  • Verifying your membership through your phone number and security question

We allow up to 3 check-ins without your card per month. If you've lost your card, a replacement can be issued for a small fee.

Do you have a dress code?

For safety, hygiene, and comfort of all members, we maintain these dress code guidelines:

  • Appropriate athletic footwear (closed-toe shoes) must be worn in workout areas
  • Clean, suitable athletic attire is required
  • Shirts/tops must be worn at all times (except in pool/sauna areas)
  • Clothing with offensive or inappropriate messaging is not permitted
  • A separate pair of clean indoor shoes is required during winter months

Management reserves the right to make determinations on appropriate attire.
